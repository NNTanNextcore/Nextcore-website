Về chúng tôi

Nextcore Software JSC

Established on Jun 15, 2022
Specializing in executing the development and maintenance of information technology projects for outsourcing partners.
The company's partners consist of large, medium, and small outsourcing companies in Vietnam, operating in all three markets: English, Japanese, and Vietnamese-speaking markets.
Continuously striving to address challenges as Pain points and create meaningful value for customers, aiming to become a reliable and long-term partner.

Vision

  • Becoming a trustworthy partner, delivering significant and valuable contributions to our partners.
  • Becoming an indispensable part, a 'family member' in understanding and resolving the 'Pain points' of our customers and partners.

Mission

  • Providing high-value software products and services.
  • Striving to offer solutions with significant added value for both customers and partners.
  • Supplying additional value beyond development, such as stable workforce, streamlined processes, consultancy, high collaboration, learning, and development opportunities.
  • Delivering progress, dedication, and accountability.
  • Demonstrating strong communication skills focused on understanding customers' needs.

Core Value

  • Harnessing and leveraging the strengths of each individual towards common goals.
  • Encouraging and fostering the formation and implementation of new initiatives and ideas.
  • Respecting, sincerely acknowledging, and transparently recognizing the contributions of each team member.
  • Striving to build a collaborative environment with strong connections among members.
  • Recognizing the human factor as the core and central element.
