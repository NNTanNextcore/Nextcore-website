# Asset provenance

All runtime assets are stored in `preview/assets/`. There are no remote fonts, libraries, images, or API requests.

## New images

`hero-corporate-v2.png` (1672 × 941) is the Hero-only background derived with OpenAI image editing from the user's approved `ChatGPT Image 09_17_53 10 thg 9, 2026.png`. Website text/navigation/buttons were removed while preserving the three-person composition, black polos and red N marks. This remains an AI-edited illustrative image, not a verified documentary photograph. The original `hero.png` remains in use by Blog; other sections were not switched to the new image.

`service-outsource.png`, `service-consulting.png`, and `service-personal.png` were generated with OpenAI for the approved three-service redesign (10 September 2026). They illustrate software development, business planning and individual technology support. These are illustrative visuals, not photographs of actual Nextcore projects or clients.

`assets/video/nextcore-danang.mp4` is an unchanged local copy of the user-supplied `pixverse-mp4-media-web-ori-5a93ec7b-a45d-486e-8aa4-e04288d43840_seed465763283.mp4` from Downloads (1920 × 1072, approximately 3.04 seconds). `assets/images/danang-poster.jpg` is a frame extracted at 1 second from this video using the browser video decoder and canvas. It is the fallback for the company introduction section.

`hero.png`, `about.png`, and `cta.png` were generated with OpenAI image generation for this prototype. They depict illustrative office/landscape scenes; the people and office are not presented as photographs of actual Nextcore employees or premises. The visual reference was the supplied approved homepage mockup.

`partner-waves.svg` is original decorative vector code. Service/stat symbols in `index.html` are original inline SVG. Technology marks are simplified text/CSS symbols for visual review, not a complete official logo asset set. No icons were copied from an external library.
